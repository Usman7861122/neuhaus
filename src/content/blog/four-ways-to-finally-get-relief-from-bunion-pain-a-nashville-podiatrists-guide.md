If [bunion](/services/bunions-toes-forefoot) pain is slowing you down, you’re not alone.

At [Neuhaus Foot & Ankle](/), we help patients throughout [Brentwood](/locations/brentwood) and across Middle Tennessee manage bunions every day, and one of the most common questions we hear is:

*“Do I have to have surgery?”*

The answer? Not necessarily.

With the right care early on, many patients can relieve bunion pain and delay, or even avoid, surgery altogether.

Let’s walk through four effective treatments that can help you stay active and comfortable.

## The Right Shoes Make a Bigger Difference Than You Think

Wearing tight, narrow, or pointed shoes is one of the fastest ways to worsen a bunion.

We often recommend switching to footwear that includes:

- Wide toe boxes to allow natural toe movement
- Soft, flexible materials that don’t press on the bunion
- Low heels to reduce pressure on the front of your foot

A simple change in footwear can significantly reduce daily discomfort and prevent progression.

## Custom Orthotics Can Relieve Pressure

[Custom orthotics](/services)aren’t just for athletes, they can be a powerful tool for managing bunions.

These inserts are designed to:

- Improve foot alignment
- Redistribute pressure away from the bunion
- Reduce pain during walking and standing

Unlike over-the-counter options, custom orthotics are tailored specifically to your foot structure, helping your feet function more naturally.

## Padding and Splints Provide Extra Support

Sometimes, small adjustments can make a big difference.

Bunion pads and splints can help:

- Cushion the bunion and reduce friction
- Keep the toe in a better position during rest
- Slow the progression of the deformity

While these aren’t permanent fixes, they can provide meaningful relief and improve day-to-day comfort.

## Physical Therapy Helps You Move Better

Bunions can affect more than just your foot, they can change how you walk and place stress on other joints.

Physical therapy can help by:

- Strengthening foot and ankle muscles
- Improving balance and stability
- Correcting movement patterns

This not only reduces pain but also helps prevent additional issues in your knees, hips, and back.

## When Is Bunion Surgery Necessary?

In some cases, conservative treatments may not provide enough relief.

If your bunion pain:

- Interferes with daily activities
- Causes significant joint damage
- Limits your mobility

Surgery may be the best option.

The good news is that modern bunion surgery techniques are more advanced than ever, often involving minimally invasive approaches and faster recovery times.

## Don’t Wait Until the Pain Gets Worse

Bunions are progressive, which means they typically worsen over time without treatment.

The earlier you address the issue, the more options you have to manage it without surgery.
