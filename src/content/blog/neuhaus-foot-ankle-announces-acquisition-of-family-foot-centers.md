**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**
**Neuhaus Foot & Ankle Announces Acquisition of Family Foot Centers and Retirement of Dr. Chapman**

Nashville, TN – Sept. 27, 2024 – Neuhaus Foot & Ankle is excited to announce the acquisition of Family Foot Centers, marking a significant step in expanding our ability to provide exceptional podiatric care throughout Middle Tennessee. Alongside this growth, we also announce the retirement of Dr. Chapman, who has been a respected and dedicated podiatrist in the community for many years.

As part of this transition, Dr. Chapman’s patients will now have the opportunity to receive podiatric care from Dr. Watkins or Dr. Walters, two highly experienced and compassionate providers who are aligned with our mission of personalized, high-quality care.

**Office Hours & Locations**
To better serve the community, office hours will be expanded, providing greater accessibility to meet the needs of our patients. Current locations will remain unchanged, ensuring continuity of care in the same convenient settings that patients are familiar with.

**Insurance Coverage**
In addition to expanded office hours, insurance coverage options will also be broadened, offering patients more flexibility and access to the care they need.

“Our acquisition of Family Foot Centers reflects our commitment to providing comprehensive, patient-centered care to more communities throughout Tennessee,” said Allen Patin, CEO of Neuhaus Foot & Ankle. “We’re excited to build on Dr. Chapman’s legacy and ensure his patients continue to receive the best possible care from our dedicated team of specialists.”

For more information about the transition or to schedule an appointment, please visit neufoot.com or call 615-220-8788. We look forward to welcoming Dr. Chapman’s patients and expanding access to high-quality foot and ankle care across the region.

**About Neuhaus Foot & Ankle:**
Neuhaus Foot & Ankle is the leading podiatry practice in Middle Tennessee, with 19 clinics dedicated to delivering top-quality, patient-centered care. Our team of skilled podiatrists is committed to helping patients maintain optimal foot health, from routine care to advanced treatment options.
