**Short answer:** the toenail fungus treatments with the best proof are prescription antifungal pills and medicated nail products used for a long enough time. Home remedies and most over-the-counter products have weak proof. Even when treatment works, a clear nail takes a year or more to grow out.

## First, make sure it is fungus

Not every thick or yellow nail is fungus. Nail injuries, psoriasis and other conditions can look similar. A podiatrist can look at the nail and, if needed, test a small sample. Treating the wrong problem wastes time and money. See [5 things we want you to know about toenail fungus](/blog/5-things-we-want-you-to-know-about-toenail-fungus) and our [toenail fungus page](/conditions/toenail-fungus/).

## Treatments that have good proof

### 1. Prescription antifungal pills

Pills such as terbinafine or itraconazole are taken for about 12 weeks for toenails. They work from the inside and generally have the best track record. Your doctor may order blood tests for liver health, and will check that the medicine is safe with your other medications.

### 2. Medicated nail paints and solutions

Prescription products you put on the nail, such as efinaconazole, tavaborole or ciclopirox, are mainly used for mild or early cases. You apply them daily, often for up to 48 weeks. They are slower, but they are a good choice if you cannot take pills.

### 3. In-office nail care

Your podiatrist can trim and thin the nail (debridement). This helps the medicine reach the fungus and makes thick nails feel better in shoes. In rare, severe cases, the nail can be partly or fully removed.

### 4. Combining treatments

Many people do best with more than one approach, for example pills plus nail care, or a medicated product plus regular debridement. Your podiatrist will choose a plan that is right for your health.

## Treatments with weaker proof

- **Over-the-counter creams and polishes:** most are made for athlete's foot on the skin. They rarely reach the fungus inside the nail. Read [are over-the-counter toenail fungus treatments effective?](/blog/are-over-the-counter-toenail-fungus-treatments-effective)
- **Home remedies** like vinegar soaks, tea tree oil or mouthwash: not enough proof that they cure the fungus.
- **Lasers:** research is still early. Lasers have been cleared to improve how a nail looks, not to cure the infection, and insurance usually does not pay for them.

## How long until my nail looks normal?

- Pills: about 12 weeks of treatment
- Nail paints: up to 48 weeks
- A toenail grows slowly, so clear new nail can take 12 to 18 months to grow in

Take pictures every month so you can see slow progress. Finish the whole course, even if the nail starts to look better.

## How to stop it from coming back

- Keep your feet clean and dry, and change socks when they get damp
- Wear shoes that breathe and rotate pairs
- Use sandals in public showers and pools
- Do not share nail clippers or files
- Treat athlete's foot early
- Ask about treating your shoes
