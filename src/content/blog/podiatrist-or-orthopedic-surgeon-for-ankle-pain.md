**Short answer:** for most ankle pain, a podiatrist is a good first stop. Podiatrists spend their whole career on the foot and ankle. An orthopedic surgeon is a good choice for major injuries, or when the problem reaches beyond the ankle.

Both kinds of doctors can help you. The best choice depends on what is wrong and how bad it is.

## What does a podiatrist do?

A podiatrist (DPM) is a doctor who trains only in the foot and ankle. They diagnose and treat problems such as:

- [Ankle sprains](/conditions/ankle-sprains/) and [chronic ankle instability](/conditions/ankle-pain-chronic-ankle-instability/)
- Tendon problems, like [peroneal tendon injuries](/conditions/peroneal-tendinitis-tendinosis-tears-subluxation/) and [Achilles tendon pain](/conditions/achilles-tendinitis-tendinosis/)
- [Arthritis in the foot and ankle](/conditions/osteoarthritis-of-the-foot-ankle-midfoot-subtalar-joint/)
- Many foot and ankle [fractures](/conditions/foot-ankle-toe-fractures/)
- Pain from flat feet, high arches or poor alignment

Podiatrists use X-rays, ultrasound and other imaging, and they can treat you without surgery or with surgery when it is needed.

## What does an orthopedic surgeon do?

An orthopedic surgeon is a doctor who treats bones, joints, muscles and tendons across the whole body. Some focus on the hip or knee. Some choose extra training in the foot and ankle. They are often involved with large injuries, complex breaks, and joint replacement.

## So which one should you see first?

**A podiatrist is usually the best first stop if:**

- You twisted your ankle and it is still sore or swollen after a few days
- Your ankle gives way or feels loose
- You have pain on the inside or outside of the ankle that comes and goes
- You have ankle pain with flat feet, high arches or bunions
- You have diabetes and new ankle or foot pain

**An orthopedic surgeon may be the better choice if:**

- The injury is large and involves the knee, leg or hip as well
- You need a joint replacement
- Your podiatrist recommends one for a complex case

Good doctors work together. If your podiatrist feels another specialist is a better fit, they will tell you and refer you.

## When ankle pain is an emergency

Do not wait for an appointment. Go to the emergency room if:

- The ankle looks bent or out of shape
- You heard a snap and cannot put weight on it
- The foot is cold, pale, blue or numb
- A bone is showing through the skin
