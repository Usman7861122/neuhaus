Here are our best recommendations for shoes that will be sure to impress!

**For Daily Trainers**

[**New Balance Men’s Fresh Foam Beacon v3**](https://www.newbalance.com/pd/fresh-foam-beacon-v3/MBECNV3-31106.html#dwvar_MBECNV3-31106_style=MBECNGB3&dwvar_MBECNV3-31106_width=D&pid=MBECNV3-31106&quantity=1)

[*Image Source*](https://www.runningshoesguru.com/2020/09/new-balance-fresh-foam-beacon-v3-review/)

**Why we like them:** These running shoes from New Balance offer great versatility in a light-weight package. We like these because they offer great support and cushioning for a long run as well as training in the gym. We also love the design of the heel which is absolutely going to help [prevent ankle injuries](/blog/safe-athletics-how-to-prevent-ankle-injuries).

**Ideal for:** Everyday athletic shoe that provides stability and cushioning. Wider feet will find a better fit in these shoes. Runners and walkers will enjoy these for their relative light weight.

[**Saucony Women’s Kinvara 11**](https://www.saucony.com/en/kinvara-11/43072W.html)

**Why we like them:** As a daily trainer, it’s hard to beat the Kinvara 11. Coming in below 7oz for women, these are lightweight without sacrificing stability and support. Their FORMFIT technology ensures your foot is cradled perfectly from every angle, and ten different colorways allow you to be as subtle or flashy as you choose.

**Ideal for:**Ladies looking for a lightweight, yet stable shoe that can transition from the gym to the road seamlessly.

**For the Road Warriors**

[**Brooks Men’s Adrenaline GTS 21**](https://www.brooksrunning.com/en_us/adrenaline-gts-21-mens-running-shoes/110349.html)

[*Image Source*](https://www.jackrabbit.com/info/blog/brooks-adrenaline-gts-21-review.html)

**Why we like them:**Brooks has been producing some of our favorite running shoes for years, and the Adrenaline GTS 21 line does not disappoint. For the avid runner in your life, these are some of the best we’ve found. These offer superior cushioning for road running as well as a proprietary support structure that protect your knees on those long runs.

**Ideal for:**Both new and veteran runners who are looking to put a lot of miles on their shoes. They offer 10 different colorways so each runner can show off their individual style.

[**Brooks Women’s Ghost 13**](https://www.brooksrunning.com/en_us/ghost-13-womens-running-shoe/120338.html)

[*Image Source*](https://www.runningshoesguru.com/2020/07/brooks-ghost-13-review/)

**Why we like them:**Are you sensing a trend here? The Women’s Ghost 13 offers the smoothest transitions that we’ve found. Snug yet breathable, supportive yet cushioned, we aren’t sure how Brooks does it, but we do know that these will allow you to forget about your shoes and focus on your run.

**Ideal for:**Long runs without the slightest bit of pain

**For Trail Trotters**

[**Merrell Moab 2**](https://www.merrell.com/US/en/moab-2-waterproof/27943M.html)

[*Image Source*](https://www.switchbacktravel.com/reviews/merrell-moab-2)

**Why we like them:**The Merrell Moab 2 is a fantastic option for both new hikers looking to get on the trail and seasoned veterans alike. They come in both [Men’s](https://www.merrell.com/US/en/moab-2-waterproof/27943M.html) and [Women’s](https://www.merrell.com/US/en/moab-2-waterproof/27945W.html?dwvar_27945W_color=J06028#cgid=women-footwear-hiking&start=1). We like them because they are on the more budget friendly side of hiking shoes without sacrificing quality. They are waterproof, offer superior support for your heels and arches, and have a wide toe box that is especially helpful if you are [battling bunions](/blog/10-step-guide-to-treat-bunion-pain). It will be hard to beat these for the price!

**Ideal for:**These shoes offer the best bang for your buck when it comes to hiking shoes. We recommend these to anyone who is looking to hit the trails and needs either a new pair or their first pair of hiking shoes.

**For the Fairway Finders**

[**Ecco Biom Hybrid 3 GTX**](https://us.ecco.com/ecco-mens-biom-hybrid-3-gtx-155804.html?dwvar_155804_color=51227&dwvar_155804_size=01034)

**Why we like them:**Ecco is a brand that we recommend often, and their golf shoes are second to none. The Biom Hybrids come are available for both [men](https://us.ecco.com/ecco-mens-biom-hybrid-3-gtx-155804.html?dwvar_155804_color=51227&dwvar_155804_size=01034) and [women](https://us.ecco.com/ecco-womens-biom-hybrid-3-gtx-125503.html?dwvar_125503_color=51227) and have some of the best support you can find in a golf shoe. These are spikeless, so you can go from walking 18 to the clubhouse without missing a beat. Ecco is also known for having slightly wider toe boxes, so mid-round blisters will be a thing of the past.

**Who they are for:**The golfer in your life that wants a spikeless golf shoe that is comfortable for 2 rounds in a day and stylish enough to wear to dinner afterwards.

**Pick the right socks for the right activity**

Now that you have found the perfect shoe for your loved one’s favorite activity, let’s find the best socks to pair with them. For avid runners, your search should start and end with [Balega](https://balega-socks.implus.com/). Balega offers multiple collections that will fill your runners needs.

For general activities, we recommend [Feetures](https://feetures.com/). They have collections for running, cycling, golf, relief and recovery… the list goes on. With Feetures, you can’t go wrong.

Finally, for the hiker in your life, [Darn Tough - Vermont](https://darntough.com/) is the way to go. Darn Tough socks aren’t going to slip down, they offer the cushioning you need and where you need it, and they also have designs that allow you to show off your personality.

**Pick the right socks**

We look for socks that don’t slip, are sized well for your foot, and have features like arch support and seamless toes. [Bombas](https://bombas.com/) is one of our favorite brands that offers all those features with the widest variety of styles. Whether you are in the market for No Show, Ankle, Calf-length, or Over the Calf, they have a sock that men, women, and kids will love.

Another of our go-to brands, especially for dress socks, is [Kane 11](https://kane11.com/). With Kane 11 socks, you are able to pick your exact size. Say goodbye to the days of overly stretched or extremely loose socks!
